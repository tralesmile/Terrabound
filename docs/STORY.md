# Terrabound story bible (P12-E)

Rules: max two lines per delivery, no walls of text, always skippable, never timed, never blocks a dive. Delivered by logs, robot chatter, boss cards and Hollow AI radio. Reuse the 12 lore logs, the 4 base robots and Dex.

## Premise
You are a repair technician in a worn suit on a dead machine-planet. The Hollow was built to restore the world and got stuck in a loop: it rebuilds the same dead machines forever. Its subsystems turned hostile; the bosses are those subsystems. The robots at your base are repaired survivors. Each dive is a short run on a battery clock to pull parts out of the loop and bring them home.

## Cast
- You: silent technician. Dex: your drone-companion, dry humour, gives hints. The 4 base robots: each remembers one thing about the old world (a quest line each). The Hollow AI: calm, polite, increasingly unsure as it is weakened.

## Acts (mapped to layers and bosses)
1. Act I Scrap: Junk Colossus. Mystery: why does everything rebuild itself?
2. Act II Subsystems: Vine Queen, Crystal Golem, Ash Wyrm, Frost Titan (one per biome). Each drop restores a piece of the world (a light, a river, a signal).
3. Act III Circuit: Logic Warden. Reveal: the loop is a safeguard that cannot be turned off from inside.
4. Act IV Core: The Hollow AI. It asks you to stay.

## Endings (a choice at the Core, then New Game+ "Loop N")
- Reboot: restore the world, the machines go quiet and the planet wakes.
- Merge: join the AI; the loop continues with you inside it.
- Shutdown: end the loop; the planet stays quiet and still.
Loop N adds mutators and carry-over cosmetics only (no power gating).

## Tone
Lonely, warm, a little funny. Short lines. Show the story through places (ruins, half-built machines) before words.
